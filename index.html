How it responds

From first sign to emergency alert

01

Sensors read health vitals

MAX30102 and MLX90614 sensors gather real-time SpO2, BPM, and Temperature readings.

02

Firebase synchronizes data

ESP32 transmits live metrics directly to the Firebase Realtime Database over Wi-Fi.

03

Web & LINE notifications trigger

The system evaluates risk conditions, updates the website dashboard, and pushes immediate alerts to your LINE account.
